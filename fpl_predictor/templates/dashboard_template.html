Cells show how easy the opponent is at that venue. Team names show the team's own season strength.

Cells add the team's own home/away strength to the opponent's: green vs green (10) is best, red vs red (2) worst.

How the colours work
  • Opponent only: the opponent's rate at that venue (xGC/M it concedes for attack, xG/M it creates for defence), ranked against the other 19 teams into bands, green easiest. Total adds the band numbers: lower is an easier run.
  • Combo: the team's own rate at that venue is banded too, green worth 5 down to red 1, and the two add up. Sum adds the combos over the gameweeks shown: higher is a better run.
